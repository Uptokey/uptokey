What it is

Uptokey is a local, OpenAI-compatible proxy at 127.0.0.1:11435/v1. Provider keys stay in your operating system's native vault — never in plaintext, never in the browser. You choose which provider or local model handles each request, and the connection status is always visible: ok, blocked, or unreported.

The app

Explore the dashboard in the browser. Keys are never in the composer. Pairing is only needed for real calls.

The proxy

127.0.0.1:11435/v1. OS vault. Works with Cline, Continue, Aider, and any OpenAI-compatible client.

The state

Stay in control of your token usage. No invented balance, no silent swap.
